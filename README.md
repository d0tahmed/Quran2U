# Quran2U

**A free Quran companion for Android. No ads, no tracking.**

Read the whole mushaf, listen to sixteen reciters with Urdu or English
tarjumah, learn Tajweed with a daily streak, listen to Stories of the Prophets
with live subtitles, keep your prayers on time with the adhan, and get through
Ramadan with Sehri and Iftar times and a khatm plan.

## Install

1. Open the [latest release](https://github.com/d0tahmed/Quran2U/releases/latest)
   on your phone and download the `.apk`.
2. Open it. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser or file manager.
3. That's it. When a new version comes out, the app tells you.

Quran2U is for 64-bit Android phones.

## The app at a glance

The app has five tabs at the bottom. Settings opens from your profile picture
at the top of the screen.

| Tab | What you'll find there |
|---|---|
| **Home** | Today's prayer times and the next prayer, the Ramadan card in Ramadan, your Tajweed streak, the newest story, continue reading and continue listening, the daily ayah, the daily quiz, quick links to Duas, Tafseer, Hadith and Qibla, and Ahmed, the helper, near the top. |
| **Listen** | Every surah with your chosen reciter, tarjumah, and the full player. |
| **Read** | The mushaf, the surah list, Tafseer, Bookmarks, Ask the Quran and Learn Tajweed. |
| **Stories** | Stories of the Prophets and the Seerah, told and recited. |
| **Library** | Liked surahs, bookmarks, downloads and your listening history. |

The app opens in a light theme (ivory paper and antique gold). A dark theme,
or "follow my phone", is in Settings.

## Read the Quran

- **The whole mushaf, built in.** All 604 pages, in three scripts: **Uthmani**,
  **Indo-Pak** and colour-coded **Tajweed**. Pages turn instantly and work with
  no internet at all.
- **Surah reader.** Read any surah ayah by ayah with its translation under it,
  and play it from any ayah.
- **More than 100 translations.** Choose any translation from Quran.com in
  Settings, or read in Arabic only. Translations you open are kept for offline
  reading.
- **Tafseer.** Ibn Kathir and Ma'ariful Quran for every ayah. Pick a surah from
  the Tafseer list and read it ayah by ayah.
- **Word Study.** Press and hold any word to see its three-letter root, other
  words from the same root, how often it appears (in Makkan and Medinan
  surahs), every ayah it appears in, and a short note from Tafsir Ibn Kathir.
- **Pick a word from any ayah.** In the surah reader, the word button next to
  an ayah lists its words. Tap one to open its Word Study.
- **Bookmarks and continue reading.** Bookmark any ayah or page. Home always
  offers to take you back to the exact page you were on.
- **Arabic text size.** Make the Arabic bigger or smaller in Settings.
- **Full screen.** Tap the mushaf page to hide everything but the page. Tap
  again to bring the controls back.

## Ask the Quran

A search box that understands feelings as well as words. Find it at the top of
the Read tab.

- **Ask about how you feel or what you're going through.** Type things like
  *"I feel lonely"*, *"exam stress"*, *"how should I treat my parents"* or
  *"waiting for my dua to be answered"*. You get the ayahs scholars usually
  point to for that situation, with hadith from the six books under
  **From the Sunnah**.
- **More than a hundred topics**, from grief, fear and anxiety to marriage, a
  new job, money worries and a hard heart.
- **Search any word or phrase.** *Dhul Qarnayn*, *honey*, *iron*, or a
  half-remembered line. The words you searched for are highlighted.
- **Go straight to an ayah.** Type `2:255` to open Ayat al-Kursi.
- **English or Urdu meanings.** Switch at the top of the screen.
- **Works offline.** Topic answers are built into the app. Searches you have
  done before still work without internet.

These answers are a starting point for reflection, not a fatwa. Always read
the full passage and its tafseer.

## Listen

- **Sixteen reciters**, each with a photo and a short profile:
  Abdul Rahman As-Sudais, Mishary Rashid Alafasy, Mahir Al-Muaqily,
  Saud As-Shuraim, Yasser Ad-Dusari, Ahmed bin Talib Hameed, Abdul Basit
  Abdus Samad, Muhammad Siddiq Al-Minshawi, Saad Al-Ghamdi, Ali Jabir,
  Bandar Al-Balilah, Nasser Al-Qatami, Muhammad Ayyoub, Badr Al-Turki,
  Salah Al-Budair and Abdul Mohsin Al-Qasim.
- **Switch reciters in one tap.** Tap a reciter in Listen. Playback carries on
  from the same ayah instead of starting the surah again.
- **Tarjumah mode.** Each ayah in Arabic, followed straight away by its Urdu or
  English translation, read aloud. Turn it on or off without losing your place.
- **Live highlighting.** The ayah being recited is highlighted on screen and
  the page follows along. You can turn it off in Settings.
- **The full player.** Previous and next surah, playback speed, a sleep timer,
  and the queue of what plays next.
- **Keeps playing.** Audio carries on in the background and on the lock screen,
  with previous, play and next on the notification. The next surah starts by
  itself.
- **A close button** on the player bar, in the app and in the surah reader, to
  put it away when you're done.
- **Downloads.** Save one surah, or the entire Quran with or without the
  tarjumah, and listen with no internet.

## Learn Tajweed

Open it from the Tajweed card on Home, or from the Read tab.

- **A lesson for every rule** in the Tajweed mushaf: Ikhfa, Idgham with and
  without ghunnah, Iqlab, Ikhfa Shafawi, Idgham Shafawi, Ghunnah, Qalqalah,
  every kind of madd, Hamzat al-wasl, the sun letters, silent letters and
  merging close letters. Sixteen in all.
- **Each lesson** explains the rule in plain words, shows its letters, and
  plays real examples from short surahs you already know.
- **Recite along.** Follow Sheikh Husary's teaching recitation or Alafasy while
  each word lights up as it is read. Slow it down to 0.5×, repeat an ayah, or
  play on through the surah.
- **Tap any word** to hear it on its own at any speed, with how to say it, its
  meaning, its root, and the tajweed inside it.
- **Focus on one rule.** Only that rule's colour shows, so you can spot every
  Ikhfa in a surah.
- **A quick check** of five questions for every rule. Your progress is saved.

## Your daily Tajweed streak

Practise a little every day, and don't break the chain.

- **A daily streak.** Earn 10 XP in a day by reciting along, listening to
  words, or passing a quick check, and the flame grows.
- **XP and levels.** Every bit of practice earns XP. Climb from **Beginner** to
  **Student**, **Reciter**, **Skilled reciter** and **Master reciter**. The
  Levels page shows the levels you've reached, where you are now, and the
  locked ones with the XP each needs.
- **A daily goal ring and three daily quests**, with bonus XP for finishing
  them.
- **Sixteen badges** to earn, drawn in 3D, including one for practising every
  day of Ramadan.
- **Streak freezes.** Every seven days in a row earns one (you can hold two).
  A freeze saves your streak on a day you miss.
- **Celebrations** when you extend your streak, level up or earn a badge, with
  a spinning 3D coin and confetti.
- **A progress page** with a 12-week calendar, your best streak and your stats.
- **Reminders that know your streak.** A nudge at the time you usually
  practise, a heads-up in the evening if your streak is at risk, and a last
  call before midnight. They stop as soon as you practise that day.
- **A streak widget** for your home screen.
- **The leaderboard.** See who earned the most XP this week and of all time,
  with a podium for the top three. Anyone can look at it. To join it, sign in
  with Google.

## Stories of the Prophets

Stories from the Quran and the life of the Prophet Muhammad, peace and
blessings be upon him. Each one is told in English in about ten minutes, and
the ayahs that tell the story are recited by Sheikh Mishary Rashid Alafasy in
between, with the Arabic and its meaning on screen.

**Episodes so far**

| # | Story | Topic |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Prophet Yunus | Prophets |
| 2 | The Hijrah | The Migration |
| 3 | Prophet Yusuf | Prophets |
| 4 | Prophet Musa | Prophets |
| 5 | Prophet Ibrahim | Prophets |
| 6 | The First Revelation | The Revelation |
| 7 | The Battle of Badr | The Battles |
| 8 | The Night Journey (Isra and Mi'raj) | The Revelation |
| 9 | The Battle of Uhud | The Battles |
| 10 | The Conquest of Makkah | The Battles |

**In the player**

- **Live subtitles** that light up word by word as the story is told.
- **Focus mode.** Tap the focus button at the top and everything goes away
  except the subtitles, on a dark screen. The screen is locked so nothing gets
  tapped by mistake. **Double tap anywhere to unlock.**
- **Transcript.** The whole story as text. Tap any line to jump to it.
- **Speed** (0.75× to 1.5×), back and forward 10 seconds, and skip part by part.
- **Sleep timer.** 10, 20, 30 or 45 minutes, or the end of the episode.
- **Subtitle size.** Make the subtitles bigger or smaller.
- **Save for offline** with one tap, **like** a story, and **share** it.
- **Up next.** When an episode ends, the next one starts by itself after a
  short countdown, unless you say "Not now".
- **Keeps playing** in the background and on the lock screen.

**On the Stories tab**

- Stories grouped by topic, with filters for each topic and for the ones you
  liked.
- **Continue listening** for stories you're part way through, and a check mark
  on the ones you've finished.
- **New stories arrive by themselves.** No app update needed. You get a
  notification when a new episode is out, and a dot on the Stories tab.

## Hadith and duas

- **The six books of hadith** (Kutub al-Sittah): Sahih al-Bukhari, Sahih
  Muslim, Sunan Abi Dawud, Jami at-Tirmidhi, Sunan an-Nasa'i and Sunan Ibn
  Majah, in English, Arabic and Urdu. They are built into the app and work
  offline. Find any hadith by its number.
- **Hisnul Muslim.** Authentic duas for every part of the day, in 131
  categories, with transliteration and references. Pick a category from quick
  chips or search the whole list.

## Prayer times, adhan and Qibla

- **Prayer times** for where you are, worked out on your phone (Karachi
  method, Hanafi Asr), with a live countdown to the next prayer.
- **A living sky** that follows the day: stars and the moon at night, sunrise
  at Fajr, birds at Dhuhr, wind at Asr and dusk at Maghrib.
- **The adhan** at Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib and Isha: the Madinah adhan by
  Sheikh Muhammad Marwan Qassas. It plays like an alarm, so you hear it even
  on silent, in Do Not Disturb and with the app closed. Set each prayer to
  **full adhan**, **a silent notification**, or **off**. A Stop button is on
  the notification.
- **Qibla compass.** Point your phone and turn until it lines up.
- **Hijri date** on Home, next to today's date. If the moon was sighted a day
  earlier or later where you live, adjust it by up to two days in Settings.

## Ramadan mode

**Before Ramadan**, Home shows a countdown: the days to Ramadan, the dua
*Allahumma balighna Ramadan* ("O Allah, let us reach Ramadan"), and a lock with
the day Ramadan mode opens. You can hide the countdown in Settings.

**Ramadan mode opens by itself 4 days before Ramadan**, so you have time to set
your reading plan, and stays through Eid and the six fasts of Shawwal. There is
nothing to switch on.

**A celebration** of lights, lanterns, a crescent and stars on the first day of
Ramadan, and of lights, balloons and dates on Eid al-Fitr and Eid al-Adha.

**Every day of Ramadan**

- **Sehri and Iftar on Home**, with the day of Ramadan and a live countdown:
  "Iftar in 2h 27m".
- **Notifications** when Sehri ends in 10 minutes, and at Iftar with the dua for
  breaking the fast. Each can be turned off.
- **A 30-day timetable** of Sehri and Iftar for your location, and a
  ready-made **post to share** on WhatsApp status and Instagram, with the whole
  month's timings.
- **The prayer widget** says Sehri and Iftar instead of Fajr and Maghrib.
- **A daily checklist**: the fast, the five prayers, taraweeh, Quran, dhikr and
  sadaqah, with a ring for the day and a 30-day calendar. A fast marked as
  missed is added to **fasts to make up**.

**Finish the Quran**

- **A khatm plan**: once (a juz a day), twice, three times, or your own number
  of pages. Home shows today's pages.
- **Pages count by themselves** when you read them in the mushaf, and you can
  add pages read from a printed mushaf. Each juz and each khatm is celebrated.
- **Tonight's juz** to listen to, recited by an imam of the Haramain (Sudais,
  Shuraim, Muaiqly and others), playing from where the juz begins.

**The last ten nights**

- The odd nights are marked, with a reminder at Isha on the 21st, 23rd, 25th,
  27th and 29th nights.
- The dua the Prophet taught for Laylat al-Qadr, and Surah Al-Qadr one tap
  away.

**Duas, ayahs and stories**

- Duas for seeing the new moon, breaking the fast, being a guest at iftar, when
  someone insults you while fasting, and Laylat al-Qadr, each with its source.
- The daily ayah switches to the ayahs about fasting, with a hadith about
  Ramadan each day.
- **Happened in Ramadan**: the First Revelation, the Battle of Badr and the
  Conquest of Makkah, on the Stories tab and the Ramadan screen.
- Ramadan questions in the daily quiz, and a Ramadan badge in the Tajweed
  streak.

**Eid**

- An Eid countdown, the Eid takbeer, and an **Eid Mubarak card** to share.
- **Six fasts of Shawwal**: tap "I fasted today" and watch the six fill up.

## Daily ayah and daily quiz

- **Daily ayah.** One ayah and one hadith each day, with a daily notification
  at the time you choose that carries the actual ayah.
- **Daily quiz.** Ten new questions every day about the Quran: surah names and
  their meanings, Makki or Madani, the order of revelation, ayah counts,
  themes, and the ayah of the day. Everyone gets the same quiz on the same day.
- **A hint on every question**, and every answer explains itself and links to
  its surah.
- **Stars, a quiz streak and a progress page** with a 12-week calendar,
  mastery for each topic and your all-time accuracy. The quiz streak counts the
  days you showed up, not the days you scored well.

## Accounts and sync

You don't need an account. Everything works as a guest.

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Guest** | Use the whole app. Your progress stays on this phone. You can still look at the leaderboard. |
| **Sign in with Quran.com** | Your bookmarks sync with your Quran.com account and the website. |
| **Sign in with Google** | Joins the Tajweed leaderboard and keeps your streak, XP, levels and badges safe if you change phones. |

**You can use both.** Quran.com keeps your bookmarks, and Google keeps your
Tajweed progress. Signing in to one never signs you out of the other.

## Settings

Open Settings from your profile picture.

- **Appearance:** light, dark, or follow your phone. Switching is a smooth
  circular reveal.
- **Default reciter**, **audio language** (Urdu or English tarjumah) and

- **Live highlighting** on or off.

- **Ahmed, the helper:** ask how to use anything in the app.
- **Report a bug:** message us on Instagram.

## Ahmed, the Quran2U helper

Ask Ahmed from the card near the top of Home, or from Settings.

- **How to use the app**, in your own words, like "how do I download a surah?".
  Answers come with a button that takes you straight to the right screen.
- **Ayahs and hadith on a topic**, like "ayahs about patience" or "I feel
  lonely", quoted word for word from the ones built into the app, with their
  references.
- **No fatwas.** For a ruling, ask a scholar you trust.
- **Anything harmful is refused.**
- **Most answers come from the app itself**, instantly and offline.

## Privacy

- **No ads, no analytics, no trackers.**
- **Your location never leaves your phone.** It is only used to work out prayer
  times and the Qibla.
- **Accounts are optional.** If you sign in with Google, the leaderboard shows
  your name, XP, level and streak to other Quran2U users, and your Tajweed
  progress is saved so you can get it back. Nothing else is stored. If you
  don't sign in, nothing leaves your phone.
- **Quran.com sign-in** is used only to sync your bookmarks.
- **Ahmed, the helper,** answers most questions on your phone. A question he
  can't answer there is sent to the Quran2U help service to be answered from
  the app's guide. Questions he turns away (harmful, fatwas, off topic) are
  noted there without your name or any id, and deleted after 30 days. Please
  don't type personal details in it.
- **Ramadan mode** keeps your checklist and khatm plan on your phone only.
