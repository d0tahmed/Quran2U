# Quran2U

**A Quran companion for Android.**

Read the whole mushaf, listen to sixteen reciters with Urdu or English
tarjumah, Stories of the Prophets with live subtitles, prayer times with a
living sky, the six hadith collections, Hisnul Muslim and a daily quiz.

## Install

1. Open the [latest release](https://github.com/d0tahmed/Quran2U/releases/latest)
   on your phone and download the `.apk`.
2. Open it. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser or file manager.
3. That's it. When a new version comes out, the app tells you.

Quran2U is for 64-bit Android phones.

## What's here

This repository holds the app's releases and the **Stories of the Prophets**
the app plays. New episodes added to [`stories/`](stories) appear in the app
on their own, with no update needed.

The app's source code is not published.

## Privacy

No ads, no tracking, no analytics. Your location is used on the phone to work
out prayer times and the Qibla, and never leaves it.
